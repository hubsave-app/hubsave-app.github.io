Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

👋 Introduction

Welcome to HubSave. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any information when you use our app.

🔍 Information We Collect

We are fully transparent about all information collected while using the app:

1. Device Information (Automatic & Anonymous):

  • Device type and model (e.g., Samsung Galaxy S23)
  • Operating system and version number (e.g., Android 14)
  • Screen resolution and device language
  • Advertising ID - you can reset this from your device settings

2. Usage Data (Firebase Analytics):

  • Number of app opens and session duration
  • Pages and features used within the app
  • Number of videos downloaded (without saving video URLs)
  • Source platform (YouTube, TikTok, etc.) - for statistical purposes only

3. Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics):

  • If a crash or unexpected error occurs, an anonymous technical report is sent
  • Reports include: error type, code path, memory state - and do NOT include any personal data

4. Advertising Data (Google AdMob):

  • The app uses Google AdMob which may collect data for personalized advertising
  • This includes: Advertising ID, ad interaction data, and basic device information
  • This data is fully managed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy
✅ We NEVER collect: Real name, email address, phone number, GPS location, contacts, photos, messages, or any personal content from your device.

📊 How We Use Data

  • Improving app performance and download speed
  • Discovering and fixing technical bugs in future updates
  • Understanding which platforms and features are most used to improve them
  • Displaying ads via Google AdMob (when enabled)
🔒 We never sell any data to any third party and do not share your data with anyone except the Google services mentioned above.

🔑 Required Permissions

The app requests the following permissions for specific and necessary purposes only:

  • Internet: To download videos and fetch data from servers
  • Storage: To save downloaded videos to your Downloads folder
  • Notifications: To show download progress and completion notifications
  • Background Service: To continue downloads when leaving the app

📁 Local Data Storage

The app stores data locally on your device only, such as:

  • Downloaded videos log (in a local database)
  • Favorites list
  • App settings (language, dark mode)
🔐 This data is never sent to any external server and remains on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes all this data.

⚖️ Personal Use & Copyright

This app is built as a "Personal Utility Tool" to help download content you own or have rights to use.

  • This app is designed exclusively for downloading your own videos or videos you have explicit permission from the content owner to download.
  • We do not encourage, support, or facilitate any form of piracy or copyright infringement.
  • You agree that you are fully legally responsible for any content you download and store.
  • Using this tool to download others' content and republish it without the original owner's permission is prohibited.
⚠️ Warning: Any use that violates copyright is your full personal responsibility. The developer assumes no responsibility for misuse.

🌐 Third Parties

The app uses the following services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics — Google Policy
  • Google AdMob — AdMob Policy

🛡️ User Rights

  • You can delete all local data by uninstalling the app
  • You can disable ad tracking from your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Advertising)
  • You can reset your Advertising ID at any time
  • The app does not share your data with any third party except the services mentioned above

👶 Children Under 13

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from this age group. If we discover a child has provided personal data, we will delete it immediately.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If significant changes are made, we will notify you by updating the date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
